Solution types

Chatbot formats tailored to the way service businesses communicate.

Website chatbots

Handle FAQs, service information, and next-step guidance directly on your website without slowing visitors down.

  • Great for fast answers on pricing, availability, locations, or process questions.
  • Supports stronger engagement on high-intent landing and service pages.

AI support chatbots

Offer instant support-style responses that help reduce delays and create a more responsive client experience.

  • Useful for pre-sales support, common objections, onboarding, and client education.
  • Maintains a helpful tone while keeping the brand experience premium.

Lead generation bots

Collect enquiry details, guide qualification, and help route prospects into the right next step automatically.

  • Strong fit for services that need screening, quoting, or appointment qualification.
  • Pairs well with campaign landing pages and high-response offers.

WhatsApp chatbot solutions

Create cleaner handoffs from website visits into WhatsApp conversations, automations, and follow-up flows.

  • Perfect for businesses that already close leads over messaging.
  • Supports response speed without changing your existing backend logic.
